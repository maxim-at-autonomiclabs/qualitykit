AEO MCP for AI discoverability

Call qualitykit.ai_audit to score how ready a site is for AI systems to crawl, extract, and cite — with evidence strings you can act on. Scores are heuristics; they do not predict citations or rankings.

What it scores

  • Crawlability and extractability of main content
  • Entity clarity (Organization / product signals)
  • Citation readiness and structured understanding
  • Topical coverage hints from the codebase

How it fits SEO MCP

Use AEO alongside SEO MCP and SEO + AEO: fix indexability first, then improve extractable answers and schema. Pair with the AEO how-to guide for a concrete session checklist.
