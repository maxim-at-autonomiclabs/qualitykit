SEO and AEO in one audit loop

QualityKit runs technical SEO and AI discoverability checks against the same codebase. You get defects (broken indexability, missing metadata) separated from opportunities (new intent pages, schema, briefs) — then advise-only diffs and build contracts your agent applies after you approve.

What it covers

  • Robots, sitemap, canonicals, titles, and structured data
  • AI readiness via qualitykit.ai_audit (crawlability, extractability, citation readiness — heuristic, not a guarantee)
  • Grow mode: why impressions may be limited and which page intents to add

Who it is for

Next.js teams and coding agents that want SEO + AEO findings where the code already lives — not another disconnected crawler dashboard for day-one fixes.
