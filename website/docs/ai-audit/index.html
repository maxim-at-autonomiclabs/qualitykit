AI Audit Mode

qualitykit.ai_audit evaluates whether AI search systems can discover, understand, extract, and cite your content — using deterministic heuristics and evidence, not marketing scores.

What it scores

  • Crawlability — robots, AI bot rules, sitemap, route inventory
  • Extractability — clear definitions, headings, answer-shaped copy
  • Entity clarity — consistent brand, audience, and schema signals
  • Citation readiness — authors, dates, verification markers, thin pages
  • Structured understanding — valid JSON-LD aligned with visible content
  • Topical coverage — gaps like comparison, use-case, docs, FAQ

Caveats

  • Scores do not predict rankings or AI citations
  • FAQ schema and llms.txt are optional assessments — never required
  • Providers (Google AI, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot) do not share one ranking model
  • Pass includeRendered + siteUrl only when you want live HTML checks
